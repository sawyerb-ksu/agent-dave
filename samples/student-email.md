# Practice input: a student email

This is a fictional email and the facts needed to answer it. Use it with the `student-email-reply` checkpoint skill if you need a small task for the final test.

## Facts supplied by the instructor

- Instructor: Dr. Rivera.
- The reading list is on the course site under **Resources → Week 9**.
- Students who cannot access the course site should contact the course support desk.
- No date, support email address or URL has been provided.

## Email

Subject: Where is this week's reading list?

Hi Dr. Rivera,

I missed class and can't find this week's reading list. Where should I look, and what should I do if I still can't open it?

Thanks,
Alex

Check that the draft answers both questions, stays under 150 words, uses Alex's name, and invents no URL, contact address or deadline. Improve the skill by requiring the direct answer in its first sentence, then `/reload` and try again.
