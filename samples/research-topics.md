# Research topics for the research-brief skill

Each fits on one page. They need the internet, because DAVE reads sources with the fetch tool.

- What are universities doing about AI policies in syllabi this year?
- How do open-weight language models compare with hosted ones for campus use?
- What does the research say about retrieval practice for studying?
- What are the current federal rules on data management plans for grant proposals?
- What are good practices for writing accessible course materials?
- What is the Model Context Protocol, and who supports it?

Ask it like this: *"Research this and write me a one-page brief: (topic)"*
